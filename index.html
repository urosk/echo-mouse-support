Getting started

Ready in four steps

  1. 1

    Allow access

    Grant Input Monitoring permission so Echo Mouse can record your actions, and Accessibility permission so it can replay them.

  2. 2

    Start recording

    Click the red Record button, then use your mouse and keyboard normally. Scrolling is recorded too.

  3. 3

    Choose how to replay

    Stop recording first and press Play for one pass, or press Play while recording to start an infinite loop.

  4. 4

    Cancel with Escape

    Press Esc at any time during playback. Echo Mouse stops immediately and releases any keys it was holding.

Playback

Once, or until you say stop

The same two buttons handle every workflow.

Play once

Click Record again to finish, then click the green Play button. The recording runs one time.

Play in a loop

While Echo Mouse is still recording, click Play. It finishes the recording and repeats it until you press Esc.

Troubleshooting

Common questions

What exactly does Echo Mouse record?

Mouse movement, left, right, and other mouse-button clicks, dragging, scrolling, keyboard presses and releases, and modifier-key changes are kept on one timeline with their original timing.

How do I stop a recording from looping?

Press Esc. Playback stops immediately. The Escape key is reserved for cancellation, so it is not recorded or replayed.

Why is mouse or keyboard input not being recorded?

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and make sure Echo Mouse is enabled under both Input Monitoring and Accessibility. If you just granted access, quit and reopen Echo Mouse.

Can Echo Mouse record passwords?

macOS Secure Input prevents keyboard capture in password fields and some security-sensitive apps. This is a system protection and cannot be bypassed by Echo Mouse.

How long can a recording be?

A recording can be up to five minutes long. Loop playback can continue indefinitely until you cancel it with Esc.

Where is my recording stored?

Your recording stays in memory on your Mac. It is replaced when a new recording starts and discarded when Echo Mouse quits.

Why does the pointer click Play at the end of every loop?

When you start looping directly from a recording, the Play click is part of that recording and remains visible at the end of every pass.
