Privacy Policy

Your actions stay yours.

Echo Mouse records and replays mouse, scrolling, and keyboard input locally, without sending your actions to us.

Effective August 9, 2026

Summary

  • We do not collect or upload your mouse, scrolling, or keyboard input.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking technologies in the app.
  • Your recording stays in memory on your Mac and is never saved to a file.
  • Echo Mouse does not connect to the internet.

Recorded input

While you are recording, Echo Mouse processes mouse movement, clicks, dragging, scrolling, key presses, and modifier-key changes locally on your Mac. The app does not upload this input to us, and we do not store it on our servers.

Information stored on your Mac

Your current recording is held only in memory. It is replaced when you start a new recording and discarded when Echo Mouse quits. The app stores one local preference indicating whether the macOS permission request has already been shown; it does not contain any recorded input.

Permissions

Echo Mouse requests Accessibility permission to observe and replay mouse, scrolling, and keyboard events. This permission is controlled by macOS and can be changed in System Settings. Echo Mouse does not use Accessibility access to read the contents of other apps. macOS Secure Input prevents keyboard capture in password fields and some security-sensitive apps.

Other apps

Input replayed by Echo Mouse becomes input in the app or service you are using. That app or service may process or store the input under its own privacy policy. Echo Mouse does not control what happens to the input after it is replayed.

Children’s privacy

Echo Mouse does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If Echo Mouse’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change takes effect. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.
